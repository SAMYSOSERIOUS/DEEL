Methode

How it works, and what it can't tell

Learn “normal”

A model learns from 2015 to August 2022 how demand follows the hour, weekday, holidays in each state, school holidays, temperature and sunshine.

Test it first

It forecasts four normal winters it never saw. Those errors set the width of every range on this page.

Forecast “no crisis”

Fed the real weather of each winter since 2022, it says what demand would have been. The gap is the saving.

Honest limits

Grid data can't say who saved, or separate thrift from closed factories, or price effects from appeals. The report goes through each one.
